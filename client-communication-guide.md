# How to talk to clients (simple English)

You do not need fancy words. Clients want to feel understood and to know what happens next.

## The 3 steps for every conversation
1. **Ask first, pitch later.**
2. **Say it back.** "So you need ___ so that ___. Is that right?"
3. **Give one clear next step with a time.** "I'll send a one-page plan and price by tomorrow 6 pm."

## First call (20 minutes)
Open: "Thanks for your time. I'll ask a few questions, then tell you how we can help. Is that okay?"

Ask these, in order:
1. What do you do, and who are your customers?
2. What problem are you trying to solve? (more enquiries, less manual work, a new product?)
3. What do you have today? (website, app, nothing?)
4. What happens if you don't fix it?
5. When do you need it?
6. What budget did you have in mind? If they don't know: "Websites usually start from ₹50,000 and apps or AI agents from ₹1 lakh. Does that range fit?"
7. Who decides?

Close: "Let me repeat what I heard… I'll send you a short plan and price by ___. Will you be the one to review it?"

## Words to use / avoid
| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| "It will take about 3 weeks." | "The sprint cadence is…" |
| "Your customers can book online." | "We'll implement a booking microservice." |
| "I don't know yet, let me check and tell you tomorrow." | Guessing |
| "That is outside this price. I can quote it separately." | Silently agreeing |

Never promise results (like "10x leads"), and never invent past clients or numbers.

## WhatsApp templates
**First reply to an enquiry (within 1 hour)**
> Hi {Name}, thanks for contacting NeoKlyn. I understand you need {what they said}. Quick question: {one question}? You can also get a free rough estimate here: https://neoklyn.com/estimate

**After the call**
> Thanks for your time today, {Name}. As discussed: {goal in one line}. I'll send the plan and price by {day, time}.

**Sending the quote**
> Hi {Name}, here is the plan and price for {project}: {summary: what, how long, how much}. Happy to answer any questions. If it looks good, we can start on {date}.

**No reply after 3 days**
> Hi {Name}, just checking if you saw my message. Happy to adjust the plan if needed.

**No reply after 7 days**
> Hi {Name}, I'll leave this guide here in case it helps later: {link}. Message me whenever you're ready.

**When the price is too high for them**
> I understand. We can start smaller: {smaller scope} for {lower price}, and add the rest later. Would that work?

## When you don't know the answer
Say: "Good question. I want to give you the right answer, so let me check and reply by {time}." Then do it.

## Before you start any project
Send a one-page agreement: what you will build, price, dates, what the client must provide, and 30 days of free fixes after launch.
