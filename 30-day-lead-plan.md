# NeoKlyn 30-Day Lead Plan (Websites · AI Agents · Mobile Apps)

Built from a review of neoklyn.com on 3 Oct 2026 plus your existing `google-business-profile-guide.md` and `social-media-promotion-guide.md` (its 14 days of posts cover days 1–14 of social; this plan adds the rest and the system around it).

Goal: 30 days of consistent activity so that you get **qualified conversations**. Lead numbers below are targets to measure against, not promises.

---

## 1. What the site analysis shows

**Strong (keep, push harder)**
- Clear promise on the home page: "Agency quality. Half the price. Twice the speed", 2–5 week delivery, senior-engineer review, and prices shown (from ₹50,000 web, ₹30,000/month).
- Many ways to contact you: WhatsApp (pre-filled message), email, a form with a "<4 hr reply" promise, an AI estimator (/estimate) and an audit (/audit).
- 6 case studies, including a small-clinic one (Sharon Physio) that matches the buyer you most want.
- Technical SEO base is in place: sitemap, robots, structured data, Google verification, Bing verification, llms.txt, Microsoft Clarity.

**Gaps that cost leads (fix in week 1)**
1. **Contact page speaks to the wrong buyer.** It says "Enterprise Intake… ambitious companies", and the budget ranges start at $5K–$15K (about ₹4 lakh+). A small business with a ₹50,000–2 lakh budget will think it isn't for them. Add a "Under ₹1 lakh / ₹1–3 lakh / ₹3–8 lakh" INR option (default INR for Indian visitors), and rename the heading to something like "Tell us what you need".
2. **Mobile app and AI agent pages have no price or example.** Add "Typical project: ₹X–Y, N weeks" and one concrete use case each (e.g. WhatsApp order-taking agent, clinic booking app). Pricing is visible for web only.
3. **Mobile app has no case study.** Your portfolio is all websites. Build 1 small demo or concept app, or write up a real one if you have it. Until then, lead mobile with the cost-and-timeline article.
4. **One draft article may not be live.** `content/insights/native-vs-cross-platform-app-development.mdx` is not in the live sitemap. Check it is published, as it is your best mobile-app search page.
5. **No lead-capture below the fold on articles.** Each insight article should end with: "Get a free estimate" button + WhatsApp link + audit CTA.
6. **Case-study numbers (+45%, +110%, +30%) need a source line** ("measured over X months by client's analytics"). Only keep ones you can back up.
7. **Check analytics.** Clarity is installed; confirm Google Analytics / Search Console are set up and that form submits, WhatsApp clicks and estimate completions are tracked as events. If you can't see them, you can't tell what works.
8. **Weekly blog routine is blocked** (network setting, see earlier). Fix this first.

## 3. Weekly rhythm (about 2 hours a day)

- **Mon:** Blog or long LinkedIn post (the routine drafts; you publish).
- **Tue–Thu:** 20 personalised outreach messages/day + 10 meaningful comments on other people's posts.
- **Fri:** Reel/Short + review requests + follow-ups.
- **Sat:** Measure (leads, replies, calls booked), adjust.
- **Every day:** reply to every enquiry within 1 hour. WhatsApp first.

## 4. The 30 days

### Week 1 (Days 1–7): Fix the funnel and set up the channels
- **D1** Fix the contact form (INR budgets, friendlier heading). Confirm the weekly blog routine works (add neoklyn.com to its allowed domains).
- **D2** Add price + one example use case to the Mobile App and AI Agents pages.
- **D3** Add the end-of-article CTA block (estimate / WhatsApp / audit) to all insights.
- **D4** Set up tracking: GA4 and Search Console, events for form submit, WhatsApp click, estimate complete, audit complete. Make a simple lead sheet: date, name, source, service, budget, status, next step.
- **D5** Finish the Google Business Profile (follow your guide). Ask all 6 past clients for a Google review using the WhatsApp templates in the guide.
- **D6** Update LinkedIn (company + personal), Instagram, X, YouTube bios using the copy in your social guide. One offer and one link in each bio.
- **D7** Build your **prospect list**: 100 businesses (40 website, 30 AI agent, 30 mobile) with owner name and a contact route. Note one specific thing wrong or improvable on each.

### Week 2 (Days 8–14): Start posting and outreach
- **D8–D14** Post the existing 14-day content from `social-media-promotion-guide.md` (Part 5).
- **D8** Publish article 1 (the Monday blog). Share on LinkedIn with a short story, not just a link.
- **D8–D14** Send 15 outreach messages/day from the prospect list (scripts in section 5). Offer the free audit.
- **D10** Post a case study (Sharon Physio) as a LinkedIn carousel: problem → what you built → result → link.
- **D12** Join 5 relevant communities (Bengaluru startup/founder WhatsApp and LinkedIn groups, Tamil Nadu business groups). Don't pitch in the first week: answer questions.
- **D14** Review: how many replies, how many audits requested?

### Week 3 (Days 15–21): Lead magnets and proof
- **D15** Promote the **Free Website Audit** (/audit) as the main lead magnet: a LinkedIn post, an Instagram Reel showing an audit result, and a pinned post on X.
- **D16** Post: "5 things your small-business website is missing" (website offer).
- **D17** Post: "What an AI agent can actually do for a clinic/school/shop" with a 40-sec screen recording of a simple WhatsApp agent demo (AI offer).
- **D18** Post: "Native vs cross-platform app: cost and timeline" (mobile offer). Link the article.
- **D19** Do 3 outreach calls/visits to businesses in your city (offer a quick on-the-spot website review).
- **D20** Ask each new lead/client for a short testimonial and permission to quote it. Add it to the site.
- **D21** Publish article 2. Email/WhatsApp everyone who has talked to you so far with a helpful link, not a pitch.

### Week 4 (Days 22–30): Convert and scale what works
- **D22** Follow up every conversation that went quiet (message 2 and 3 in the sequence below).
- **D23** Offer a **limited-slot fixed-price starter package** (e.g. "5-page business website, ₹X, 2 weeks, 3 slots this month"). Scarcity must be real, so only say 3 slots if you really have only 3.
- **D24** Run one small paid test if budget allows: **₹300–500/day for 7 days** on Google Search ("website developer Bangalore", "mobile app development company Bangalore") or Meta/Instagram to the audit page. Stop and review after spend of ₹2,000–3,000 if no enquiries.
- **D25** Post a "behind the build" video: AI generates, you review. This is your difference, so show it.
- **D26** Contact 10 past or current clients: ask for referrals (offer a thank-you, e.g. a discount on their next work) and for a LinkedIn recommendation.
- **D27** Pitch 2 guest-post/podcast/community-talk opportunities ("How AI cuts the cost of building software").
- **D28** Publish article 3.
- **D29** List what worked: which channel gave replies, calls and leads. Double down on the top 2, drop the bottom 2.
- **D30** Review numbers against targets (below). Plan month 2.

## 5. Copy-paste scripts

**Cold message, website (WhatsApp / Instagram / LinkedIn)**
> Hi {Name}, I looked at {business} online. {One specific observation, e.g. "your site takes ~6 sec to load on mobile" / "I couldn't find a way to book an appointment"}. I run NeoKlyn in Bengaluru, we build fast business websites in 2–5 weeks. Happy to send you a free 5-point audit of your site, no obligation. Want it?

**Cold message, AI agent**
> Hi {Name}, I saw {business} gets a lot of enquiries on WhatsApp/phone. We build AI agents that answer common questions, take bookings and follow up with leads, so your team spends less time on repetitive replies. Would a 20-minute call to see where it could save you hours each week be useful?

**Cold message, mobile app**
> Hi {Name}, I saw you're building/running {business}. Do you have an app planned? We build iOS + Android apps from one codebase in weeks, fixed price, and you own the code. I wrote a short guide on cost and timeline: {link}. Happy to give you a rough estimate for your idea.

**Follow-up 1 (day 3):** "Just checking you saw my message. Happy to send the audit/estimate straight away if useful."
**Follow-up 2 (day 7):** Share one useful thing (article, tip). No ask.
**Follow-up 3 (day 14):** "Closing the loop. If timing isn't right, no problem. I'll leave this link in case it helps later: /estimate."

**Discovery call questions (20 min):** What's the goal (leads, sales, saving time)? What exists today? Who uses it? Deadline? Budget range? Who decides? → send a one-page proposal within 24 hours.

## 6. Targets and tracking (30 days, adjust after week 2)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Outreach messages sent | 300+ |
| Replies | 30+ (10%) |
| Audits / estimates requested | 15+ |
| Discovery calls | 8–10 |
| Proposals sent | 5–6 |
| Clients won | 1–2 |
| Google reviews added | 5+ |
| Posts published | 20+ across platforms, 3 blog articles |

Review every Saturday. If outreach replies are under 5%, rewrite the opening line with a more specific observation. If audits are requested but calls don't happen, shorten the audit follow-up. If posts get views but no enquiries, add a clearer CTA.

---

## 7. Do not do
- Do not claim results, client names or numbers you can't verify.
- Do not send the same message to hundreds of people. Personalise the first line.
- Do not buy followers or leads lists. Do not spam WhatsApp groups.
- Do not spend more on ads before the funnel fixes in section 1 are done.
